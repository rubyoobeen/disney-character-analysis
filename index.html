Disney Character Gap Cards

Characters people love more than their movies. Gap = character popularity score − movie score (both z-scores). Bigger gap, rarer card. Click a card to flip it.

Character art from the Disney API (disneyapi.dev). Characters the API doesn't cover show a monogram instead.
A character's gap is the average across the films it appears in; the back of each card breaks it down per film.
